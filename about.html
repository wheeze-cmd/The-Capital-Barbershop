THE CAPITAL

ABOUT US

The lore.

Founded in 2024, The Capital Barbershop was built with a passion for precision grooming, modern style, and exceptional customer experience. More than just a place for haircuts, The Capital was created to provide a premium space where confidence, craftsmanship, and self-expression come together. From clean fades and classic cuts to beard grooming and modern styling, every service is delivered with attention to detail and professionalism.

At The Capital, we believe that a great haircut is more than appearance — it is a reflection of confidence and personality. Our goal is to create an atmosphere that is welcoming, stylish, and comfortable for every client who walks through our doors. With skilled barbers, quality products, and a commitment to consistency, The Capital continues to grow as a modern barbershop dedicated to helping clients look sharp and feel their best.

BARBER

The man. The myth.
The legend.

Kuya Johnny is the founder and sole barber behind The Capital Barbershop. Established in 2024, the shop was built from his passion for barbering, precision grooming, and creating a space where clients can feel confident and comfortable. What started as a personal vision became a modern barbershop focused on quality service, consistency, and attention to detail.

As the only barber of The Capital, Kuya Johnny personally handles every haircut, beard trim, and grooming service with dedication and craftsmanship. His approach combines classic barbering techniques with modern styles, ensuring that every client leaves looking sharp and feeling their best.

Through hard work, discipline, and genuine passion for the craft, Kuya Johnny continues to grow The Capital Barbershop into more than just a place for haircuts — but a space built on confidence, style, and community.
